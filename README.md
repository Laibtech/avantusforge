# AvantusForge — Production Website

> **"Forging Custom Software, Modern Websites, and Scalable Digital Solutions."**

AvantusForge is a custom software and digital solutions company built to help ambitious businesses build, automate, modernize, and scale their digital operations across the UK, USA, UAE, Kuwait, and Pakistan.

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 16 (App Router)
- **Language**: TypeScript (Strict typing)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 & Semantic CSS
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (Montserrat for UI & body, Lora for editorial emphasis)
- **Animations**: Framer Motion (reduced-motion accessible)
- **Icons**: Lucide React & Semantic Symbols
- **Code Quality**: ESLint 9 & Next.js config

---

## 🎨 Design System & Color Palette

**Strict Rule**: ZERO PURPLE. The brand identity is built on a sophisticated burgundy & warm gold aesthetic:

| Token | Hex Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Burgundy | `#750F2C` | Primary brand color, key buttons, icons & active indicators |
| Deep Burgundy | `#52091E` | Section backgrounds, card highlights & focus states |
| Dark Burgundy | `#3D0717` | Footer background, code windows & terminal accents |
| Premium Gold | `#C99A3D` | Key accent lines, status dots & micro-highlights |
| Light Gold | `#E1C27A` | Subtle gold text highlights & dark background accents |
| Main Background | `#FCFAF8` | Clean, premium warm canvas background |
| Soft Burgundy | `#F7EEF1` | Alternating section backgrounds & subtle card pills |
| Border Subtle | `#E8DDE0` | Elegant hairline borders |
| Dark Text | `#191416` | High-contrast readable typography |
| Muted Text | `#6F6568` | Secondary copy & technical metadata |

## 📁 Homepage Sections

1. **Header Navigation**: Sticky navbar with custom two-angled-bar geometry mark in Burgundy & Gold, smooth anchor navigation (`#services`, `#solutions`, `#work`, `#about`, `#insights`, `#contact`), and mobile menu.
2. **Hero Section**: Eyebrow, headline, supporting copy, dual pill action buttons, and custom 3D perspective device screen with floating cards (`🌐 Websites`, `⚙️ Automation`, `🛒 Ecommerce`).
3. **Stats Bar**: Market regions (UK, USA, UAE, Kuwait, Pakistan), 50+ Projects Delivered, 4+ Years Experience, 5 Markets Served, 24/7 Support.
4. **Problem Section**: 3 core business hurdles (`⚙` Manual Processes, `▣` Outdated Digital Presence, `◉` Disconnected Systems).
5. **Services Section**: 6 core service cards (01 Custom Software, 02 Business Automation, 03 Websites, 04 Ecommerce, 05 SaaS, ERP & CRM, 06 Digital Growth).
6. **Process Section**: 6-step engineering approach (01 Discover, 02 Plan, 03 Design, 04 Build, 05 Launch, 06 Scale) with flow arrows.
7. **Industries Section**: 6 industry vertical cards (♡ Healthcare, ⌂ Real Estate, 🎓 Education, ▣ Professional Services, 🛒 Retail & Ecommerce, ♧ Hospitality & Events).
8. **Work Section**: Featured case studies (Caremon, FAMA Bags, SheeshToTonight) with styled image backdrops and direct case study links.
9. **Why AvantusForge Section**: 4 core business pillars (⬡ Business First, ♙ Built Around You, ∞ One Partner, ▥ Built to Scale).
10. **Technology Section**: Understated technology pills (Next.js, React, Node.js, PHP, Laravel, WordPress, Shopify, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Prisma).
11. **Testimonials & FAQ Section**: Two-column layout pairing authentic client testimonials (Shehzod Ghafoor, Rashid Khan, AB Raza) with a 5-item Framer Motion accordion.
12. **Final CTA Section**: Deep Burgundy gradient with horizontal alignment and gold conversion button.
13. **Footer**: 4-column sitemap, company overview, direct email (`info@avantusforge.com`), international presence, and compliance links.
